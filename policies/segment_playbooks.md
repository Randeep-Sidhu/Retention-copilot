# Segment playbooks

Preferred order of offers by contract and internet service. Choose the first offer in the list that the customer is eligible for; skip any offer whose eligibility rule fails. Company guidance, not a statistical finding.

## Month-to-month, Fiber optic
Preferred order: 1. Tech support trial (TECH_SUPPORT_TRIAL); 2. Term upgrade (TERM_UPGRADE); 3. Loyalty discount (LOYALTY_DISCOUNT); 4. Service check-in (SERVICE_CHECKIN).
Fiber customers most often need reliability and support, so service-fit offers come before price offers.

## Month-to-month, DSL
Preferred order: 1. Term upgrade (TERM_UPGRADE); 2. Tech support trial (TECH_SUPPORT_TRIAL); 3. Loyalty discount (LOYALTY_DISCOUNT); 4. Service check-in (SERVICE_CHECKIN).
DSL customers respond well to a longer commitment with support included before a plain discount.

## Month-to-month, no internet (phone only)
Preferred order: 1. Term upgrade (TERM_UPGRADE); 2. Loyalty discount (LOYALTY_DISCOUNT); 3. Service check-in (SERVICE_CHECKIN).
Phone-only customers have no add-ons to offer, so commitment and price offers come first.
